🌿 Hidden Karnataka

Discover places where nature meets history and every corner tells a story.

Beyond the Famous Destinations

Karnataka is not just about its famous cities. It also has mysterious caves, ancient temples, beautiful rivers and peaceful landscapes. These lesser-known destinations reveal the hidden beauty and rich heritage of the state.

01. Yana Caves – The Land of Rocks

Located in Uttara Kannada, Yana is famous for its enormous black limestone rock formations. Surrounded by dense forests, this destination attracts nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.

Special Attraction: Unique rock formations and green forests.

02. Lakkundi – A Treasure of History

Lakkundi is a historic village known for its beautiful temples, ancient stepwells and detailed stone carvings. It reflects the artistic excellence of the Chalukyan period.

Special Attraction: Ancient temples and stepwells.

03. Maravanthe – Where Sea Meets River

Maravanthe is a beautiful coastal destination in Karnataka. The Arabian Sea lies on one side of the highway while the Souparnika River flows on the other side.

Special Attraction: Beautiful coastal scenery.

04. Manjarabad Fort – The Star-Shaped Wonder

Located near Sakleshpur, this unique fort was built during the reign of Tipu Sultan. Its star-shaped design and surrounding hills make it a fascinating historical place.

Special Attraction: Star-shaped architecture.

05. Sahasralinga – A Spiritual Wonder

Situated in Uttara Kannada, Sahasralinga is known for numerous Shiva lingas carved into rocks along the Shalmala River. It is an important spiritual destination.

Special Attraction: Ancient riverbed carvings.

06. Badami – The Ancient Heritage

Badami is famous for its magnificent rock-cut cave temples and ancient Chalukyan architecture. Its historic monuments showcase the architectural achievements of Karnataka.

Special Attraction: Rock-cut cave temples.
