Art & Traditions of Karnataka

Karnataka is famous for its traditional dances, folk performances, puppetry and handicrafts.

Yakshagana

A traditional theatre form combining dance, music, colourful costumes and storytelling.

Dollu Kunitha

An energetic folk dance performed with large drums, mainly associated with the Kuruba community.

Veeragase

A powerful traditional dance inspired by stories of Lord Veerabhadra.

Kamsale

A devotional folk dance performed using metal instruments called Kamsale.

Gombegalu Traditional Dolls

Karnataka is famous for Channapatna wooden toys and traditional dolls displayed during Gombe Habba.

Togalu Gombeyaata

A traditional shadow-puppet art form using colourful leather puppets.

Handicrafts

Mysore paintings, sandalwood carvings, Bidriware and Channapatna toys represent Karnataka's craftsmanship.
